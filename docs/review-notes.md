## App Review Notes (EN — wkleić w App Store Connect)

Digi Witch is a daily guide to symbolic traditions: horoscope, Moon calendar, tarot, numerology, Chinese zodiac, I Ching and a dream dictionary, with a private journal. It is presented as entertainment, learning and self-reflection, not prediction (stated on the first screen and in Settings). We believe it offers a meaningfully different experience from typical apps in this category (Guideline 4.3(b)):

1. **Every result is explainable.** Each numerology number shows the method, the inputs, every arithmetic step and the algorithm version. Sky data (Moon phases, eclipses, Mercury retrograde, ascendant) is computed on the device from astronomical formulas and verified against an ephemeris library in our tests, not taken from a feed.
2. **No AI and no server-generated readings.** All texts are original, written for this app and shipped inside it. An answer to a question put to the cards is assembled by fixed rules from the drawn cards' texts. The same input always gives the same explanation.
3. **Sources and rights are stated in the app.** Library articles on the history of tarot and numerology end with a bibliography; Settings → "Sources and rights" names the origin of the card images (Pamela Colman Smith, 1909 deck, public domain).
4. **Private by design.** No account, no login, works offline. Birth data, names and the journal never leave the device; Settings → "Delete all data" removes everything. The journal links an entry to the exact result it reflects on and can be exported.
5. **No fatalistic or advice content.** Cards such as Death or The Tower are framed as change, not doom. The app states that it is not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice, and Settings → Help lists crisis lines for each language.

**Monetisation.** One small banner ad (Google AdMob) in the free version, shown only after the Google UMP consent form and, on iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt. One non-consumable in-app purchase, `pro_lifetime` ("Digi Witch Pro"): removes ads and unlocks two extra tarot spreads. No subscription. "Restore purchases" is in Settings → Digi Witch Pro. Pro works regardless of the ad consent choice.

**How to test** (no login needed; set the device language to English):
1. Open the app → "Let's begin" (the birth date is optional).
2. Tab "Numbers" → enter a birth date, e.g. 29 May 1975 → "Calculate" → expand "Life path" to see the step-by-step trace.
3. Tab "Tarot" → draw a spread; each card opens its full description and image source.
4. Settings (top bar) → Privacy policy, Delete all data, Digi Witch Pro (purchase and restore), Ad privacy settings.

Home-screen widgets (iOS 17+, Android) show the day's text written by the app; they collect nothing.
