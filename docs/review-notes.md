# Szkic notatek dla recenzenta (App Review Notes / Play) — DO UZUPEŁNIENIA przed wysyłką

> Uczciwy opis: aplikacja dotyczy numerologii i tarota (kategoria „fortune telling” w 4.3(b)). Nie ukrywamy tego.

**EN (dla Apple):**

This app covers numerology and tarot, positioned as learning about symbolic traditions and self-reflection, not prediction. We believe it offers a meaningfully different experience (Guideline 4.3(b)):

1. **Explainable calculation engine** — every numerology result shows the method name, inputs, each arithmetic step, master-number rule and the algorithm version. The convention is documented in-app (Library → "Jak liczymy"), including how it differs from other schools.
2. **Explicit sources and rights** — in-app articles on the history of numerology and tarot with bibliography; content provenance is stated in Settings.
3. **Private reflection journal** linking a specific result (frozen with its calculation trace and content version) to the user's own writing; export and delete in-app.
4. **Offline, no account, no AI chat, no data leaves the device.**
5. **No fatalistic content** — e.g. Death/Tower cards are framed as change, not doom; disclaimers that this is not medical/psychological/financial advice.

No login is required. To test: open the app → "Zaczynamy" → tab "Liczby" → enter 29051975 → "Oblicz" → expand "Droga życia" to see the step-by-step trace.
